Zombie Bash Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version

Zombie Bash does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, coins, tower XP, Armory unlocks, store purchases made with in-game coins, sound setting and a saved battle) is kept only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Network

The game runs entirely offline. It does not send data to any server.

Children

Because the game collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the game is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the game's App Store page.
