**Description** (4000):
The dead are walking, and every road leads to your base. Hold the line across 250 levels in five worlds: farmland, graveyards, frozen tundra, toxic swamp and a fallen city of skyscrapers.

BUILD YOUR DEFENSE
Place machine gunners, snipers, mortar teams, cryo cannons, flamethrowers, tesla coils, gunboats, submarines, helicopter gunships, fighter jets and farms. Every map has a different road, so every level needs a new plan.

THREE UPGRADE PATHS FOR EVERY TOWER
Tap any tower to upgrade it in battle. Each one has three paths with five tiers, and every upgrade changes how it looks and fights:
• The Machine Gunner becomes a laser cannon and a map-wide Doom Ray, a walking Siege Mech with rocket pods, or a concrete fortress that fires in every direction.
• The Sniper goes fully automatic, or becomes a railgun that pierces every zombie in a line.
• The Farm grows into a plantation, a bank that pays interest, or a fortified safe haven.
Pick your path: a tower can push one path all the way to tier 5 and add up to two tiers of a second.

EARN XP IN THE ARMORY
Towers earn XP for every kill. Spend it in the Armory to unlock the most powerful upgrades for good.

KNOW YOUR ENEMY
Shamblers, runners, armored riot zombies, tunnelers that burrow under your guns, crows and bats that fly over your defenses, necromancers that raise skeletons, plague doctors that heal the horde, bloaters that burst into crawlers, and huge bosses at the end of every world.

MORE
• Spin the globe to pick your world
• Store with perks and one-use supplies: airstrikes, cryo bombs and supply drops
• Day, night, rain, snow and fog
• Plays offline. No ads. No accounts. No data collected.
